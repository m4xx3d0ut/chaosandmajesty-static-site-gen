The demo theme is intentionally small so you can replace it without digging through hundreds of lines of styling. Here are a few quick wins:

1. Duplicate `examples/demo-site/assets/modern.css` and adjust the color variables at the top.
2. Swap the `styles` array in your YAML file so pages load your stylesheet.
3. Update any assets referenced in the Markdown files to match your colors or illustrations.

For teams that prefer utility classes, you can even pipe the generated HTML into your favorite framework—RadioStatic does not enforce a runtime.

Need a terminal-style hero? Keep the `useTerminalConsole: true` flags in the configuration and customize the copy inside the Markdown sections.
