  <div class="blog-article-content">
    <p>The demo theme is intentionally small so you can replace it without digging through hundreds of lines of styling. Here are a few quick wins:</p>
<ol>
<li>Duplicate <code>examples/demo-site/assets/modern.css</code> and adjust the color variables at the top.</li>
<li>Swap the <code>styles</code> array in your YAML file so pages load your stylesheet.</li>
<li>Update any assets referenced in the Markdown files to match your colors or illustrations.</li>
</ol>
<p>For teams that prefer utility classes, you can even pipe the generated HTML into your favorite framework—RadioStatic does not enforce a runtime.</p>
<p>Need a terminal-style hero? Keep the <code>useTerminalConsole: true</code> flags in the configuration and customize the copy inside the Markdown sections.</p>
