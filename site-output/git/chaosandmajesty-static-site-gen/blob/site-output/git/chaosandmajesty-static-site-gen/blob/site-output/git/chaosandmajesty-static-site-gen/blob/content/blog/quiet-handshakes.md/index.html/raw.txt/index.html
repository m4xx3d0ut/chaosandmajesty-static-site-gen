  23. <p>Phrack Inc.</p>
  24. <p>Volume One, Issue 7, Phile 3 of 10</p>
  25. <hr>
  26. <p>The following was written shortly after my arrest...</p>
  27. <p>The Conscience of a Hacker</p>
  28. <p>by</p>
  29. <p>The Mentor</p>
  30. <p>Written on January 8, 1986</p>
  31. <hr>
  32. <p>Another one got caught today, it&#39;s all over the papers. &quot;Teenager
  33. Arrested in Computer Crime Scandal&quot;, &quot;Hacker Arrested after Bank Tampering&quot;...
  34. Damn kids. They&#39;re all alike.</p>
  35. <p>But did you, in your three-piece psychology and 1950&#39;s technobrain,
  36. ever take a look behind the eyes of the hacker? Did you ever wonder what
  37. made him tick, what forces shaped him, what may have molded him?
  38. I am a hacker, enter my world...
  39. Mine is a world that begins with school... I&#39;m smarter than most of
  40. the other kids, this crap they teach us bores me...
  41. Damn underachiever. They&#39;re all alike.</p>
  42. <p>I&#39;m in junior high or high school. I&#39;ve listened to teachers explain
  43. for the fifteenth time how to reduce a fraction. I understand it. &quot;No, Ms.
  44. Smith, I didn&#39;t show my work. I did it in my head...&quot;
  45. Damn kid. Probably copied it. They&#39;re all alike.</p>
  46. <p>I made a discovery today. I found a computer. Wait a second, this is
  47. cool. It does what I want it to. If it makes a mistake, it&#39;s because I
  48. screwed it up. Not because it doesn&#39;t like me...
  49. Or feels threatened by me...
  50. Or thinks I&#39;m a smart ass...
  51. Or doesn&#39;t like teaching and shouldn&#39;t be here...
  52. Damn kid. All he does is play games. They&#39;re all alike.</p>
  53. <p>And then it happened... a door opened to a world... rushing through
  54. the phone line like heroin through an addict&#39;s veins, an electronic pulse is
  55. sent out, a refuge from the day-to-day incompetencies is sought... a board is
  56. found.
  57. &quot;This is it... this is where I belong...&quot;
  58. I know everyone here... even if I&#39;ve never met them, never talked to
  59. them, may never hear from them again... I know you all...
  60. Damn kid. Tying up the phone line again. They&#39;re all alike...</p>
  61. <p>You bet your ass we&#39;re all alike... we&#39;ve been spoon-fed baby food at
  62. school when we hungered for steak... the bits of meat that you did let slip
  63. through were pre-chewed and tasteless. We&#39;ve been dominated by sadists, or
  64. ignored by the apathetic. The few that had something to teach found us will-
  65. ing pupils, but those few are like drops of water in the desert.</p>
  66. <p>This is our world now... the world of the electron and the switch, the
  67. beauty of the baud. We make use of a service already existing without paying
  68. for what could be dirt-cheap if it wasn&#39;t run by profiteering gluttons, and
  69. you call us criminals. We explore... and you call us criminals. We seek
  70. after knowledge... and you call us criminals. We exist without skin color,
  71. without nationality, without religious bias... and you call us criminals.
  72. You build atomic bombs, you wage wars, you murder, cheat, and lie to us
  73. and try to make us believe it&#39;s for our own good, yet we&#39;re the criminals.</p>
  74. <p>Yes, I am a criminal. My crime is that of curiosity. My crime is
  75. that of judging people by what they say and think, not what they look like.
  76. My crime is that of outsmarting you, something that you will never forgive me
  77. for.</p>
  78. <p>I am a hacker, and this is my manifesto. You may stop this individual,
  79. but you can&#39;t stop us all... after all, we&#39;re all alike.</p>
  80. <p>The Mentor</p>
